About ShareBite

A community-driven platform dedicated to connecting surplus food with those who need it most, fighting hunger and reducing waste.

Our Mission

We believe no good food should go to waste while people are hungry. ShareBite provides a seamless bridge between restaurants, individuals with leftover food, and NGOs or community members who can utilize it.

  • Reduce Food Waste

    By redirecting edible surplus, we minimize the environmental footprint of food waste in landfills.

  • Nourish Communities

    Ensure high-quality, fresh food reaches vulnerable populations efficiently and safely.

  • Empower Giving

    Make it as easy as clicking a button for businesses and locals to act philanthropically in their neighborhood.

The Problem We're Solving

Roughly one-third of the food produced in the world for human consumption every year gets lost or wasted. We're here to change that narrative locally.

How ShareBite Works

Post Surplus

Donors list available food with quantities and pickup times.

Connect

NGOs and receivers browse listings and request meals.

Collect

Coordinated pickup ensures food reaches precisely who needs it.
